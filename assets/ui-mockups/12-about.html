Data sources and references
Every reading on Aira AI comes from public, open APIs. We do not modify, average, or re-publish raw measurements.
CPCB Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring System · 28 active stations in Maharashtra, hourly readings via the official CCR API.
CPCB National AQI Computation Method (2014) · the official NAQI formula, not the US EPA formula.
WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines (2021) · reference for health advisories and safe thresholds.
Indian Medical Association · Air Pollution and Public Health guidance, 2023.
