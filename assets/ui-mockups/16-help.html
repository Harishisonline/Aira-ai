Help center

Ask Aira anything about the air around you.

Aira is the air quality assistant for Aira AI. This page tells you what Aira can help with, what it can't, and how it uses your data. Read this once and you'll know exactly what to expect.

In scope

What Aira is great for

Aira is grounded in live air quality data, the National AQI formula, your selected profile, and your recent advisory history. When you ask something in these areas, Aira gives a tailored, cited answer.

Live AQI for your area

Current air quality, the 6 pollutant readings (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, O3), and which bucket the AQI falls into (Good, Satisfactory, Moderate, Poor, Very Poor, Severe).

"What's the AQI in Bandra right now?"

7-day forecast for your place

Linear regression on the last 30 days of readings, with a confidence band. Predictions for the next 7 days at the same place. Not longer than 7 days — the model is calibrated for that window only.

"When will Borivali's air be cleanest this week?"

Health advice for your profile

Tailored to your selected health profile: Healthy adult, Child, Elderly, or Asthmatic. Aira translates AQI numbers into what they mean for you, not what they mean on average.

"Is it safe for my son to play cricket tomorrow?"

Station comparison and method

Which station near you is cleanest right now, why AQI is the way it is, and how the National AQI formula and the forecast model work. All grounded in the data or the methodology page.

"Is Bandra worse than Borivali today? Why?"
Out of scope

What Aira can't help with

Aira is a narrow assistant. It will decline questions outside its scope and suggest where to find an answer instead. Decline messages are short, never moralize, and always offer a redirect.

General knowledge
History, science unrelated to AQI, geography, math, coding help. Aira won't answer these. Try a general-purpose assistant for that.
Medical diagnosis
Aira translates AQI into general health implications. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe, or replace a doctor. For personal medical advice, consult a qualified professional.
Forecasts beyond 7 days
The model is calibrated for 7 days. Aira will refuse to predict beyond that and say so. For long-range weather context, try a weather service.
Indoor air quality
Aira reports outdoor AQI from CPCB monitoring stations. It does not have indoor sensor data. For indoor air, you need a separate indoor monitor.
Other states or countries
v1 only ships with Maharashtra data. Aira will tell you that and stop. Other states are coming in a later release.
Real-time events
Aira reads the live feed but doesn't have news, social media, or fire data. It can't tell you why a spike happened unless the cause is obvious from pollutant patterns. For incident context, see local news.
How Aira answers

Citations, your profile, and frozen context

Every Aira reply cites the data behind it. Aira's behavior is shaped by three things: the data it has access to, the profile you selected, and the area the chat was opened in.

What Aira does on every reply

  • Cites every factual claim with a small chip pointing to the source: live reading, 7-day forecast, or methodology
  • Tailors to your health profile — child, asthmatic, elderly, healthy adult. Switch profiles in Settings → Health profile.
  • Uses frozen context per chat — each chat remembers the location and profile you had when you opened it. Open an old chat and Aira still answers in that context.
  • Refreshes live data per turn — AQI and forecast numbers are re-fetched every message so they stay current
  • Decay: refuses out-of-scope questions in one sentence and offers a redirect — never preaches, never moralizes

Example reply with citations

Is it safe for my son to play cricket this Saturday morning in Borivali?
Yes, Saturday morning is a good window. Borivali's AQI is forecast at 135 (Moderate) at 8am, peaking to 158 by afternoon [forecast: 7-day, model v1-linreg-30d]. PM2.5 will be the limiting pollutant, around 58 ug/m3 [forecast: 7-day]. For an 8-year-old with mild asthma, an hour of outdoor play between 7-10am is safe. Avoid the 2-5pm peak [forecast: 7-day].
Sources used: get_7day_forecast(borivali) · get_recent_advisories_for_user(5) · user.health_profile = "child, mild asthma"
Privacy

What Aira sees and what it stores

Aira runs on our servers using a third-party language model (Groq). Your messages and the chat's frozen context are sent to the model provider to generate a reply. We store every chat session in your account, encrypted at rest. We do not use your chats to train any model.

  • Data Aira sees per message: your selected location, your health profile, the last 5 advisories you opened, the relevant AQI/forecast/methodology data for your question.
  • Data Aira does NOT see: your email, your password, your billing info (v1 has no billing), or anything from outside the Aira AI app.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your user row, all chat sessions, all messages, and all advisory history. Hard delete, no soft-retention.
  • Daily limit: 50 messages per user per day. After that, Aira will tell you to come back tomorrow. v1 cap; relaxed in v2.
